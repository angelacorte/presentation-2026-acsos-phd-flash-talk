Paper — Towards Collective Robotic Operating Systems through Aggregate Computing

Robot swarms are heterogeneous, they move and fail, and they must adapt without a centralized point of coordination. Programming them one robot at a time does not scale.

With Aggregate Computing the collective is programmed as a whole — and the same program runs decentralized on every device.
